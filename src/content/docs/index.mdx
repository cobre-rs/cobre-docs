This is **Cobre Documentation** — the mathematics and algorithm behind its
SDDP-based hydrothermal dispatch, together with how the software implements them.
It brings together the mathematical formulations, the algorithm, and worked
examples with the practical guide to installing, configuring, and running the
software. The Cobre **code is the ground truth**; these chapters are kept in
step with it.
