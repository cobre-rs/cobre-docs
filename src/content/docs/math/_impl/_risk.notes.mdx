Non-normative software behavior for risk measures — what Cobre computes, beyond
the equations above. This tab states the weighting Cobre applies and references
the methodology body
([§7](/math/risk-measures#7-cut-generation-with-risk-measures)) for the weights
it derives rather than restating them.

### Applied CVaR weights

Cobre applies the weights $\mu^*$ of
[Risk Measures §7](/math/risk-measures#7-cut-generation-with-risk-measures) to
the cuts of every backward pass, to the first stage's openings in the training
lower bound, and to every node of the exact nested upper bound of an enumerated
forward pass.
